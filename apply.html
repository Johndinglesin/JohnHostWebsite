Application process

How to obtain and operate a free instance.

Overview

Instances are issued by application and reviewed individually. Submit one request per applicant, wait for confirmation, and you will receive panel credentials once approved.

Steps

  1. Complete the instance request form (available from the link below).
  2. Your application is reviewed individually.
  3. On approval, the instance is provisioned and panel credentials are issued.
  4. Connect with the provided address and begin hosting.

Included with every instance

  • Hosting at no cost; no billing details are required.
  • Two (2) network ports per instance.
  • DDoS mitigation on all traffic.
  • Automated daily backups on shared storage.

Acceptable use

Use of an instance is subject to our Rules and Terms of Service. Review the two-port limit, backup usage, and prohibition on reselling before applying.
